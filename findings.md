# Key Findings

## 1. Restaurant Distribution
Bikaner had the highest number of restaurant listings among the locations analyzed, with 1,666 restaurants.

## 2. Popular Cuisine Entries
North Indian,Chinese was the most frequently occurring cuisine entry, with 6,471 restaurant listings.

## 3. Restaurant Chains
Domino's Pizza had the highest number of branches in the dataset, with 442 listings.

## 4. Highest-Rated Locations
Mylapore, Chennai had the highest average rating among locations with more than 50 rated restaurants, with an average rating of 4.23.

## 5. Cost Analysis
BBK_Chattarpur, Delhi had the highest average cost for two among the locations analyzed, at approximately ₹800.

## 6. Highest-Rated Cuisine Entries
Ice Cream,Desserts had the highest average rating among cuisine entries with more than 100 restaurants, at 4.36.

## 7. Highly Rated and Highly Reviewed Restaurants
The analysis identified restaurants with ratings of 4.5 or higher and at least 1,000 ratings. Several branches of NIC Natural Ice Creams, Corner House Ice Cream, and The Grand Sweets and Snacks appeared among the results.

## 8. Rating and Cost Insight
Mylapore, Chennai showed a strong combination of rating and cost, with an average rating of 4.23 and an average cost of ₹313 for two across 94 rated restaurants.
